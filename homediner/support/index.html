Common questions

The verification code didn’t arrive

Check your spam folder. You can resend the code once a minute; use the newest one.

I forgot my password

On the log-in screen tap “Forgot password?”, enter your email, and use the code we send to set a new password.

Change my password

Tap your avatar → More → Change password.

Will my plans move to a new phone?

Yes. Plans and your dishes belong to your account; sign in with the same account to see them.

Delete my account

Tap your avatar → More → Delete account. Your account and all its data are deleted right away and can’t be recovered.

See the guide again

Tap your avatar → How it works.
