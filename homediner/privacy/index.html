Home Diner

Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

Home Diner is an app for planning family meals and shopping lists. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, where it is stored, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password (stored only as a secure hash; we never see it), and the name you enter.
  • What you create in the app: meal plans and shopping lists (including ticks, amounts and “eaten” stamps), your versions of dishes, dishes you add yourself, and your tags.
  • Photos you add: uploaded only when you take or choose a photo for a dish. Photos are kept in private storage that only your account can read.

The app asks for camera or photo library access only when you tap to take or choose a photo, and does not read your other photos.

Language, units and some display settings stay on your phone. A copy of your plans is also kept on your phone so the app works offline.

What we don’t do

  • No ads, no advertising or analytics SDKs, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.
  • We do not access your contacts, location or data from other apps.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your plans and dishes in sync across your devices.
  • To email you verification codes for sign-up and password reset. We do not send marketing email.
  • To answer support emails you send us.

Where it is stored and who can access it

We use these service providers to run the app. They process data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase: accounts, database and photo storage, on servers in the United States (AWS us-west-2).
  • Amazon Web Services (SES): sends verification emails and processes your email address to do so.
  • Apple: distributes the app through the App Store and TestFlight, under Apple’s own privacy policy.

The database uses row-level security, so each account can read and change only its own data. All connections use HTTPS.

When you share a shopping list, it goes to the app you choose (for example Messages or WeChat) and is then handled under that app’s terms.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data until you delete your account. To delete it, tap your avatar → More → Delete account. Once confirmed, your account, plans, dishes, tags and photos are deleted from our database and storage right away and cannot be recovered. Backups at our providers may keep a copy for up to 30 days before it is removed automatically.

You can also email support@boapplab.com to access, correct or delete your data.

Children

Home Diner is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above, and tell you in the app about important changes.
