PawLink Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

This policy explains what PawLink collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. PawLink does not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.

What we collect

Account: your email address, password (stored encrypted by our sign-in provider), display name, @username and zip code.

Pets: what you enter about your pets — name, photo, species, breed, sex, birthday, weight, intro, pedigree details (breeder, sire, dam, registration) and co-owners.

Activity: your posts and photos (with the date each photo was taken, which you choose), comments, likes, reposts, follows, family and friend connections, mentions, and reports and blocks you make.

Device: a push notification token if you allow notifications. We don't collect your device's location, contacts, or advertising identifier.

App errors: when the app runs into an error, it sends us a technical report — the error message, the screen it happened on, the app version and build, and your iOS version — linked to your account, so we can fix it. We don't use it for anything else.

Who can see it

Other PawLink users can see your pets' profiles, your public posts, comments and likes, your display name and @username, and your pets' family and friend circles.

Posts marked "Only owners can see" are shown only to the pet's owners. Their photos are stored at unlisted web addresses; someone who was given such an address could open the photo.

Your email address and zip code are never shown to other users. On the Nearby map your pets appear at a random point inside your zip code.

People you block can't see your pets or content, and you won't see theirs.

How we use it

To run the app: showing your pets and posts, suggesting family connections from pedigree details, placing pets on the Nearby map, sending the notifications you've allowed (likes, comments, mentions and requests about you and your pets, and at most one push a day about pets you follow), fixing errors the app reports, and keeping the community safe by reviewing reports and removing content or accounts that break our Terms.

Services we use

Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and photo storage (United States). Google Maps shows the map and turns zip codes into map areas; only zip codes are sent to Google, never your account details. Push notifications go through Expo and Apple Push Notification service. These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete a post, a comment or a pet at any time, and delete your whole account in Settings → Account → More; that removes your profile, pets you own alone, posts, comments, likes and follows. A pet you co-own passes to its other owner. Photos that no longer belong to any pet or post are removed from storage within a few days. Reports about content may be kept for a limited time to keep the community safe.

Children

PawLink is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, get a copy of, or delete your data. You can correct and delete most of it in the app; for a copy of your data or anything else, contact us.

Changes and contact

We'll tell you in the app about significant changes to this policy. Questions or requests: support@boapplab.com.
